Mid-Point Knowledge Check
Scenario 1 of 3

The Cinema Snack Counter

The Setup

A movie theater sells popcorn in three sizes: Small for ₹150, Medium for ₹250, and Large for ₹270.

🎤

Cold-Call Prompt

"What is the theater doing here? Why does the Medium even exist?"

The Strategy

The Decoy Effect

The Medium is a decoy. It is deliberately priced so close to the Large that it makes the Large feel like an absolute bargain (just ₹20 more for double the popcorn!). It shifts the buyer's internal debate from "Do I want to spend ₹270 on popcorn?" to "Well, I'd be stupid not to upgrade."
