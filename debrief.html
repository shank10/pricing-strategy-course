Modern PM Toolkit · 15 min

AI in Pricing, Category, and Messaging

As Product Managers, how should we leverage AI in our pricing strategies? The key is understanding the strict boundary between Quantitative Optimization (Machine Learning) and Qualitative Execution (LLMs).

❌ The LLM "Prompting" Trap

It is tempting to open ChatGPT and type: "Act as a pricing strategist. My cost is ₹100, competitors charge ₹150. What should my price be?" This is a dangerous oversimplification. LLMs are linguistic prediction engines; they do not calculate live market elasticity, margin waterfalls, or two-sided liquidity. Relying on an LLM prompt to dictate your pricing methodology dilutes the mathematical rigor required to protect a company's bottom line. LLMs cannot set your price.

🧠 Machine Learning (The Math)

Used for dynamic optimization, clustering, and predictive data modeling.

  • Dynamic Yield Management: (e.g., Uber, Swiggy) Using Reinforcement Learning to adjust prices second-by-second based on live geospatial supply and demand imbalances.
  • Category Segmentation: Using unsupervised learning (like K-Means Clustering) to group customers based on usage telemetry to identify hidden WTP (Willingness to Pay) cohorts.
  • Propensity to Churn: Predictive modeling to alert PMs when a proposed price hike is statistically likely to trigger a mass exodus of a specific customer profile.

💬 Large Language Models (The Messaging)

Used for synthesis, positioning, and value communication.

  • Value Proposition Tailoring: Once the ML model identifies a high-WTP cohort, PMs use LLMs to rapidly generate and A/B test marketing copy and packaging that justifies the premium price.
  • Competitor Intelligence: Scraping and summarizing rival earnings calls and release notes to instantly map changes in the competitor's "Feature Fencing" strategy.
  • Sentiment Analysis: Parsing millions of social media mentions and support tickets to gauge user backlash following a price increase or shrinkflation event.
🎤 Trainer says "Use Machine Learning to find the numbers. Use LLMs to find the words to justify those numbers."
