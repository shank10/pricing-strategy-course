The Architectural Takeaway

In a two-sided marketplace, your product isn't the physical ride. Your product is the reliability of getting a ride.

  • Rationing, not Gouging: Surge pricing intentionally destroys demand from price-sensitive users to guarantee liquidity for price-insensitive users during peaks.
  • Buying Supply: In extreme events (Monsoons or Competitor attacks), you must temporarily override economic margins to subsidize drivers. He who controls supply dictates the market.
  • Brand as a Constraint: Pure uncapped algorithmic pricing (8.0x) optimizes short-term GMV but triggers catastrophic churn and regulatory backlash.
