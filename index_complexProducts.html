The MBA Takeaway

  • Predictability over Price: Airlines gladly trade low upfront costs for flat, predictable Operating Expenses (OpEx).
  • Incentive Alignment: GE now makes money when the engine flies, not when it breaks.
  • The Data Monopoly: By owning the maintenance, GE collects the engine telemetry, allowing IoT to slash repair costs—savings GE keeps entirely.

The Power-by-the-Hour Dynamic

Airlines pay a flat fee per year (Bars). GE accrues massive, locked-in lifetime profit (Area).
