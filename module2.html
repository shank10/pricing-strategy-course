Module 2 Post-Mortem

Final Debrief: Why pricing is dictated by psychology and distribution, not just math.

Phase 4 of 4

Lesson 1: The Margin Waterfall

In multi-tier distribution, the gross margin is an illusion. Your true pricing power is dictated by how much of the channel you can bypass.

  • Manufacturers are price-takers, not price-makers, when they don't own the end-customer.
  • If you squeeze the retailer (mechanic), they will steer the consumer to a competitor. The channel controls the shelf.

Lesson 2: Psychological Architecture

In retail, math doesn't drive consumer behavior; perception does. Shrinkflation is a calculated response engineered to prevent sticker shock.

  • Price Elasticity > Volume Elasticity: Consumers notice a ₹15 increase immediately, but rarely notice a 15% reduction in powder density.
  • When facing a cheap Private Label threat, sacrificing weight to hold your psychological price point is the only way to protect market share.
